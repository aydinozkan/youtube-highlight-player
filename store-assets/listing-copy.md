## Detailed description

**Stop scrubbing through YouTube videos looking for the good part. Let the video tell you where it is.**

YouTube Highlight Player reads the "most replayed" data YouTube already
collects for every popular video — the same curve you see under the seek
bar — and uses it to play back only the sections other viewers actually
watched the most. No AI guessing, no manual editing: just the video's own
audience data, put to work.

**How it works**

Turn on "Play Selection Only" and the video jumps straight to its
highest-engagement moments, skipping the rest automatically as you watch.
Turn it off anytime to go back to the full video — nothing is downloaded,
edited, or re-uploaded. It's your normal YouTube player, just smarter
about where it spends your time.

**No heatmap? No problem.**

Plenty of videos don't have "most replayed" data yet — new uploads,
smaller channels, lower view counts. For those, YouTube Highlight Player
falls back to the video's own chapters (creator-added or auto-generated),
so you can pick exactly which sections to watch. Videos with both get a
simple tab switcher to flip between the two.

**Built to stay out of your way**

- A compact, glassmorphic control panel appears right next to the player
  — collapses to a small pill when you don't need it front and center.
- See exactly how much time you're saving, updated live.
- Works the moment you land on a video — no setup, no account, no
  configuration required.
- A native toggle button lives right in YouTube's own player controls
  too, if you'd rather not glance at the panel at all.

**Privacy, plainly**

This extension has no backend server of its own. It doesn't sell data,
and it never sends anything about what you watch — no video ID, title, or
history — to anyone. Full privacy policy: [privacy-link]

**Open source**

The full source code is public — don't just take the privacy claims
above on faith, go read exactly what the extension does:
https://github.com/aydinozkan/youtube-highlight-player
